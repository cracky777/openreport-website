Open Report › AI data analysis

Ask your data in plain words

Type a question about your data, in any language, and Open Report answers with the figures and the chart that shows them.

Ad-hoc analysis without SQL

  • Ask “which region grew the most this year?” and read the answer, then ask the next question.
  • The AI writes the query on your semantic model, so a revenue figure means the same thing in every answer.
  • Each answer can show its chart, its table, or a comparison with last year.
  • Keep an answer: add it to a report in one click, or ask for a whole report and the AI builds it page by page.

On your databases and files

PostgreSQL, MySQL, SQL Server, Azure SQL, Oracle, Snowflake, BigQuery, Databricks, ClickHouse and Redshift, or a CSV or Excel file dropped into the chat.

With the rules you already set

  • Row-level security applies to the AI as to any reader: it only sees the rows that user may see.
  • The administrator decides what the AI may read: field names only, cached aggregates, or query results.
  • Plug in the model you want, including a local one, so the questions never leave your servers.

Where a number comes from

Ask the AI where a figure comes from: it answers from the lineage stored in OpenMetadata, column by column, back to the source tables.

Questions

What is AI ad-hoc analysis?

Answering a one-off question about your data by asking it in plain words, instead of writing SQL or waiting for someone to build a report.

Is it natural language to SQL?

Yes, but on a semantic model rather than on raw tables: the AI picks dimensions and measures defined once, so joins and calculations stay correct.

Which languages can I ask in?

Any language the AI model you plug in understands. Open Report never reads your question with keyword lists.

Is it free?

Yes. The self-hosted edition is free and open source under the GNU AGPL-3.0, AI assistant included. You bring the AI model, or a free local one.
