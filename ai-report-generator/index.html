Describe the report, the AI builds it

Say what the report is for, in your own words, and Open Report builds it on your data: pages, charts, layout and look.

From one sentence to a report

  • “A sales report for the management team” is enough: the AI asks which pages you want, or decides for you.
  • Each page answers one question, with the charts that suit it: trends, rankings, maps, comparisons with last year.
  • A designer pass then gives the whole report its look: colours, fonts, layout and the page menu.

Then it is yours

  • Move and resize the visuals by hand, or ask for a change in the chat.
  • Every change can be undone, and nothing is saved until you click Save.
  • Open the report in the editor whenever you want full control.

From a database or from files

Connect a database, or drop CSV and Excel files into the chat: the AI joins them, builds the model and then the report. Accounting files such as a French FEC become a ready-made finance report.

Built on your model

The AI uses the measures and joins of your semantic model, and any new measure it needs comes as a card you approve.

Questions

Can it generate a report from an Excel file?

Yes. Drop the file into the chat with your request: the AI builds the model and the report from it.

Can I edit the report afterwards?

Yes, by hand in the same window, in the full editor, or by asking the AI for changes.

Does it write SQL?

No. It works on your semantic model; the engine compiles the SQL, so a revenue figure is computed the same way in every report.

Which AI model does it need?

One with tool calling: a local model through Ollama or LM Studio, an OpenAI-compatible API, Anthropic or your Claude subscription. See self-hosted AI analytics.
