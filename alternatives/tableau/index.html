Open Report vs Tableau

Tableau is one of the most capable tools for visual exploration. Open Report is younger and narrower, but it is open source, free to run, and its AI assistant can run on your own servers.

Where Tableau is stronger

  • Visual exploration with years of refinement: calculations, table calculations, maps, analytics.
  • A very large community, training and a job market built around it.
  • Many more connectors, and its own fast extract engine.

Where Open Report fits better

  • No licence per reader: share a report with a hundred people and it costs nothing more.
  • Ask questions in plain words, or describe a report and let the AI build it, with the model you choose.
  • A semantic model with joins, measures and row-level security defined once for every report.
  • Readable, forkable code: nobody can withdraw a version you have installed.

Questions

Is there a free, open source alternative to Tableau?

Yes. Open Report is free and open source under the GNU AGPL-3.0, self-hosted with Docker, with no limit on users or reports.

Can Open Report open Tableau workbooks?

No. Reports are rebuilt on Open Report's semantic model, by hand or by describing them to the AI.
